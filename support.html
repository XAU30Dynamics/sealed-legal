Subscription

SEALED Monthly renews through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or from the app's own Settings → SUBSCRIPTION. Everything you sealed stays on your phone either way. Restore a purchase from the subscription page inside the app.
