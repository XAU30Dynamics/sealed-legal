One line

Sealed is a workout habit tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch with one honest ritual: press a wax seal on your day, and the impression only forms as complete as you earned.

Boilerplate

Sealed turns the day into one object. Three arcs feed it — TRAIN, FUEL and BASE — and at the end of the day you press a wax seal that forms only as full as you earned. Sealed days lock forever; there’s no going back to tidy them up. Eighty percent still holds the day, beat your plan and the wax comes up gilded, and an optional pact pairs two people whose streak only advances when both seal. Built solo in the UK for iPhone and Apple Watch. £3.99 a month, seven days free.

Why

From the founder. I’d used every tracker going and they all had the same problem: the number at the end of the day was whatever I wanted it to be. I wanted something I couldn’t argue with. A seal felt right — it’s an old idea, it’s final, and it’s beautiful when it’s earned. The rule underneath is simple: the impression forms as full as you earned, and once it’s pressed, that’s the day.
